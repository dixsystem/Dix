DIX Beta Rewards — Official Rules

Effective date: June 2026 · Launch edition: one (1) week, starting from the date this promotion is announced on DixSystem's official channels.

1. What this is

DIX Beta Rewards — Launch Edition is a one-time, one-week promotional giveaway run by DixSystem (Spain) to encourage real-world testing and feedback for DIX. The exact start and end date of the entry window is announced on DixSystem's official channels when the promotion launches. During that window, DixSystem gives away twenty (20) lifetime DIX licenses (Linux or Windows, each winner's choice) among qualifying participants.

This promotion is independent from, and not linked in any way to, DIX Atlas. Atlas data is collected anonymously and is never used to verify, select, or contact participants in this promotion. See Privacy Policy, Section 5.

2. No purchase necessary

Entering this promotion is always free. Downloading and trying DIX's free demo (three free analyses, no payment required) is enough to take part — neither entry method in Section 3 requires buying anything.

3. How to enter

Submit one entry through the form on this site during the one-week entry window. Either of the following qualifies — you only need one, not both:

  • A — Share your result. Try the free DIX demo, then submit the form with a link to a public post (X, Instagram, or TikTok) showing your real before/after score, tagging @DixSystem and using the hashtag #DixBeta.
  • B — Answer the feedback test. Try the free DIX demo, then submit the form answering the short 5-question feedback test about your experience.

For method A, the post must be public and remain accessible until winners are announced. Private accounts, deleted posts, or posts removed before verification do not qualify.

4. Eligibility

  • You must be 18 years or older.
  • Employees or contractors of DixSystem and their immediate family members may not participate.
  • One entry per person. Duplicate entries from the same person are merged into one.
  • Open internationally, subject to local laws. It is your responsibility to confirm this promotion is lawful in your jurisdiction before entering.

5. Prize

Twenty (20) lifetime DIX licenses, one per winner, for the platform (Linux or Windows) chosen by each winner. The prize is non-transferable, cannot be exchanged for cash or any other product, and has no cash value. If a winner already owns a license, DixSystem will upgrade or extend it at no cost, at the winner's request.

6. Winner selection and notification

After the entry window closes, DixSystem reviews every entry received. If twenty (20) or fewer valid entries are received, every valid entry wins. If more than twenty valid entries are received, twenty winners are selected using a public, verifiable random draw (e.g. random.org with a publicly shared seed). Winners are announced on DixSystem's official channels within seven (7) days of the window closing.

Each winner is contacted using the contact details provided on their entry form. If a winner does not respond within fourteen (14) days of being contacted, DixSystem may select an alternate winner from the same window's valid entries.

7. Verification and disqualification

DixSystem reserves the right to verify any entry and to disqualify entries that are fraudulent, fake, automated, or that violate the platform's own terms of service (e.g. purchased engagement, fake accounts, duplicate submissions). Social posts (method A) are checked manually for public visibility and authenticity; feedback answers (method B) are checked for genuine, non-spam content.

8. Data collected

The entry form collects your name, a contact (email or social handle), your chosen platform, and either your post link (method A) or your test answers (method B). This data is used solely to run and verify this promotion, is stored separately from any other DixSystem mailing list, and is deleted no later than ninety (90) days after the window closes, except for winners' records, kept for the lifetime of their license. You may request access, correction, or erasure of your data at any time by contacting privacy@dixsystem.com, in accordance with GDPR.

9. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, DixSystem is not liable for any loss or damage arising from participation in this promotion, including but not limited to issues with third-party platforms (X, Instagram, TikTok, Lemon Squeezy) used to enter or verify entries. DixSystem is not responsible for entries lost due to platform outages, account suspensions, or connectivity issues outside its control.

10. Changes and withdrawal

DixSystem may modify or discontinue this promotion at any time, effective for future editions, by updating this page and announcing the change on its official channels. Changes do not affect a winner already selected under the rules in effect at the time of their edition.

11. Governing law

This promotion is governed by the laws of Spain. Any dispute that cannot be resolved informally shall be submitted to the courts of Spain, without prejudice to mandatory consumer protection rights under EU law.
